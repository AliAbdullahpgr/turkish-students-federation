# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Turkish students who are living, studying, or preparing to study in Pakistan. They need a trustworthy introduction to Pakistan, a clear understanding of the Turkish Student Association, and practical guidance for navigating student life in the country.

## Product Purpose

MSL Pakistan helps Turkish students feel informed, connected, and at home in Pakistan. The site explains the association's identity and work, publishes practical guidance as approachable blog content, and makes events, news, publications, and ways to participate easy to discover.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, resourceful, and proudly Pakistani. The experience should feel culturally grounded and memorable without becoming formal, severe, or visually heavy.

## Anti-references

Avoid black or dark-dominant interfaces, anonymous institutional templates, dense walls of guide text, generic corporate styling, and navigation that merges news with evergreen student guidance.

## Design Principles

1. Help newcomers orient themselves quickly.
2. Express Pakistan through confident green-and-white visual identity and culturally relevant imagery.
3. Turn institutional information into warm, human, scannable stories.
4. Keep news, guidance, events, and publications distinct and easy to navigate.
5. Make every interaction work comfortably across mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive image alternatives, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
